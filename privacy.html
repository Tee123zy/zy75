Privacy Policy

Zeal Booking

The booking enquiry form at tee1234567zy.vercel.app, operated by ZEAL Production. Last updated 1 October 2026.

ZEAL Production is a cinematic video and photography studio based in Kuala Lumpur. This page explains the only data this site collects: what you submit through the booking enquiry form.

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising trackers. This policy covers exactly what the code does, nothing more.

1What we collect

When you submit the booking enquiry form, we receive:

  • Name and email — so we can reply to your enquiry.
  • Service type — the kind of shoot you're enquiring about.
  • Date and time — when you'd like the shoot to happen.
  • Location — where the shoot would take place, if provided.

We don't use cookies, analytics, or any tracking script on this site.

2How we use it

Submitting the form sends one notification email to the studio, containing the details above and a confirmation link. Nothing is written to a calendar automatically.

If the studio confirms the booking by opening that link, the same details — your name, the service, the date/time, the location, and your email as a contact reference — are used to create a single event in the studio's Google Calendar. That link expires after 7 days and can only be used once.

3Who else sees it

Two outside services process this data on our behalf, strictly to deliver the features above:

ResendDelivers the notification email. See Resend's privacy policy.
Google Calendar APIStores the confirmed event, once the studio approves it. See Google's privacy policy.

We don't sell or share your details with anyone else.

4How long we keep it

The form has no database. Your submission exists only as the content of the notification email and, if confirmed, the resulting calendar event — both of which the studio can delete at any time, the same way any email or calendar entry is deleted.

5Your choices

To see, correct, or ask us to delete information you've submitted, or to ask any other question about this policy, contact the studio directly at jerryteeziyang@gmail.com.

6Changes

If this form starts collecting or sharing data differently, this page will be updated to say so before the change takes effect.
